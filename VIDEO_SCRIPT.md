# Demo video script (as spoken)

**01 · PROBLEM · The hook**  
In the challenge data, Apetit's draft 2025 climate report puts Scope 3 emissions 314 times too high. Nobody cheated. Someone typed tonnes as kilos. That's what happens when climate data is handled by hand.

**02 · PROBLEM · The blind spot and the deadline**  
And there's a second problem you can't see: the biggest farm emission source, drained peat, isn't counted at all. From January 2027, the GHG Protocol says it must be. And Apetit's science-based targets are due that December. The clock is running.

**03 · SOLUTION · What Navigator does**  
So we built Navigator. It checks every number, finds what's missing, and tells you who should act. And a named expert approves everything. It all runs on the challenge's synthetic data. The methods are real.

**04 · DEMO · Add one number**  
Let me show you. Someone enters a rapeseed purchase: 40 kilos, for 17,240 euros. Navigator checks it instantly. That's 431 euros per kilo, a thousand times the normal price. So it's tonnes, not kilos. One click fixes it.

**05 · DEMO · 133 errors, reviewed by a person**  
Now scale it up: 133 errors in the 2025 data. Wrong units, slipped decimals, kronor booked as euros, double postings. Each shows what it was, what it should be, and why. The reviewer accepts, and it's logged with their name. Tested on Apetit's labelled errors: 96 out of 100. The baseline: 52.

**06 · DEMO · Peat: the blind spot, made actionable**  
Now the blind spot. Peat is 59 percent of contract-farm emissions, but just 23 of 356 farms make half of them. Navigator plans the advisers' route: about 470 kilometres, 8 days. One winter. Each farm gets a one-page plan. The adviser checks the soil on site, one click, and the estimate is verified.

**07 · DEMO · The cheapest way to the targets**  
The targets. One click: Find cheapest plan. Every SBTi target met, 2030 and 2035, at about 39 euros per tonne. And it's honest: 11 measures need scaling, so it flags them for validation.

**08 · DEMO · Weather risk in euros**  
It prices weather risk too: bad harvests cost about 465 thousand euros a year. Buying peas from both Finland and Sweden cuts the expected pea shortfall cost by 44 percent.

**09 · DEMO · Ask Navigator**  
And anyone can just ask. “Where do I enter a farm's pea yield?” It takes you straight there. Every answer comes from our own tables, with its sources, in English, Finnish or Estonian.

**10 · DEMO · A person approves**  
Last step: a named expert approves each part. Four of four. Only now are the numbers ready for the report, and the board.

**11 · IMPACT · What Apetit gets**  
So what does Apetit get? A checked, complete base year, before the 2027 deadline. 23 farm visits instead of 356. The cheapest route to every target. And one command that rebuilds every number in about two minutes, built to run on Microsoft Fabric.

**12 · IMPACT · The ask**  
Our ask is simple: a pilot this winter, with your field advisers, on real data, starting with those 23 farms. Correct numbers. And a clear plan for who does what. That's Navigator.
