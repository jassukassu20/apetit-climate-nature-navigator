# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

Open `docs/index.html` (or the live link) before going on stage. Type a name in **Reviewer** first. Test at 1280×720; zoom out (Ctrl −) if needed.

| # | Time | Screen / click | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | **Brief**, top | "Apetit's climate report is built from thousands of hand-typed lines. Navigator checks them and turns them into decisions. Here is the chain." |
| 2 | 0:15 | Click step 01 → **Data check** | "Your draft 2025 report overstates Scope 3 314 times: kilowatt-hours read as megawatt-hours, tonnes typed as kilos. We found 133 errors in 2025, each shown as *was → should be* with the reason." |
| 3 | 0:35 | Click **Reject** on one card, point at "2025 inventory after review" | "The expert decides. Reject a fix and the inventory changes immediately, and the decision is logged with a name. On last year's labelled errors we score 96 out of 100." |
| 4 | 0:55 | **Farms & peat** | "The biggest blind spot is drained peat: 59 % of farm emissions, and the calculator leaves it out. 23 of your 356 farms make half of the emissions." |
| 5 | 1:10 | Click a ringed farm on the map → Finnish plan → **Lohkot ovat turvetta** | "Your advisers get a route: about 470 kilometres, eight days, done this winter. Each farmer gets one page in Finnish. The adviser checks the soil on site, and the estimate becomes a verified number before the new land standard applies on 1 January." |
| 6 | 1:35 | **Targets** → **Find cheapest plan** | "Scope 1+2 and FLAG are on track for 2030. For 2035 you need the peat and rapeseed programmes scaled up. Navigator finds the cheapest plan, about €39 per tonne, and flags anything beyond its design estimate for validation." |
| 7 | 1:55 | **Ask Navigator** → a suggested question | "Ask anything in any language. It answers only from these tables and cites its sources. In production it runs on Azure OpenAI." |
| 8 | 2:10 | Back to **Brief** | "Nothing reaches the report until a named expert approves it. We propose a pilot with your advisers this winter." |
